The NVIDIA Inception badge was retrieved October 10, 2026 from a public member
website. Its original geometry is retained; the neutral background is removed,
the dark lettering is white for the hero, and NVIDIA green is preserved. The
membership line is separate from the investor row. Program information:
https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/.

Wordmarks preserve the original letter shapes. Artwork is displayed in white
on the dark hero, including a monochrome version of EF's wordmark. IIT Bombay uses its
institute emblem with a separate readable name label. The two backer assets
are the same artwork used by the user-provided Invariant reference.

SVGs extracted from public HTML have explicit white fill/color so they render
without the source website's CSS. Raster derivatives remove the neutral
background and convert the original mark to white. IIT Bombay's derivative
isolates the blue emblem from the source's white panel. No logos were redrawn.

All SVGs were parsed and inspected for scripts, external dependencies and
embedded foreign objects. All nine local assets were decoded and visually
inspected at their display sizes. The marquee retains pause, offscreen and
reduced-motion behavior, with alt text on the first copy only.

The opening reveal uses `public/brands/dirac-mark.png`, a transparent white
emblem extracted from the connected logo shape in the existing `public/Logo.png`.
The original emblem geometry is retained; its surrounding raster glow is
removed for a clean reveal against the dark hero. Existing header artwork is
unchanged.
